Privacy Policy: Field Finder for NetSuite

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy covers the Field Finder for NetSuite browser extension.

Summary

Field Finder for NetSuite is a read-only tool that helps people work in NetSuite. It does not collect, sell, or share your data. Everything it does happens in your browser, between you and your own NetSuite account.

What the extension accesses

  • NetSuite pages you open. To do its job, it reads field labels and IDs on the page you have open and, if your role allows it, field details from your account's Records Catalog. It does this with your own NetSuite login, so it can only see what your NetSuite role already allows.
  • Settings. Your preferences for the extension are saved in your browser's extension storage.
  • Records Catalog details. It may keep field descriptions from your account's Records Catalog in your browser for up to one day so its tooltips are fast.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. The only network requests it makes are to your own NetSuite account.
  • It does not create, change, or delete NetSuite records.
  • It does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not sell or transfer data, use it for purposes unrelated to its single purpose, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Where data is kept, and for how long

Settings and the data described above stay in your browser until you clear them or remove the extension, or (for Records Catalog details) until they expire after one day. Removing the extension removes its stored data.

Children

The extension is a business tool and is not directed to children.
